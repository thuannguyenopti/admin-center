# Stride Company Context

This document provides background information about Stride that can be used across all AI-powered features to ensure consistent brand voice, messaging, and context.

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** Stride

**Industry:** Running Events & Athletic Experiences

**Founded:** 1983

**Mission:** To create exceptional running experiences that inspire athletes of all levels to challenge themselves and celebrate their achievements.

## What We Do

Stride organizes premium running events, from local 5Ks to major marathons, in scenic locations across the country. We focus on creating memorable race-day experiences that combine:

- **World-class event organization** with professional timing, clear course markings, and comprehensive safety measures
- **Scenic routes** through beautiful landscapes that showcase the best of each location
- **Community atmosphere** that welcomes runners from first-timers to seasoned athletes
- **Post-race celebrations** with medals, refreshments, and community gathering spaces
- **Digital engagement** through our event platform, training resources, and runner community

## Target Audience

### Primary Audiences
1. **Recreational Runners** (Ages 25-45)
   - Running for fitness, health, and personal achievement
   - Looking for well-organized, enjoyable race experiences
   - Value community and celebration over competition

2. **Serious Amateur Athletes** (Ages 30-50)
   - Training for personal records and specific time goals
   - Appreciate professional course design and accurate timing
   - Seek challenging yet achievable race experiences

3. **First-Time Racers** (Ages 20-40)
   - New to organized running events
   - Need clear information, encouragement, and accessible events
   - Value supportive, welcoming atmosphere

### Secondary Audiences
- **Families** looking for group activities and youth races
- **Corporate teams** participating in team-building events
- **Charity runners** supporting causes through athletic fundraising

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- **Encouraging**: We motivate runners to push their limits while celebrating every achievement
- **Professional**: We demonstrate expertise in event organization and runner safety
- **Inclusive**: We welcome athletes of all abilities and experience levels
- **Energetic**: We convey the excitement and joy of running and racing
- **Authentic**: We speak as fellow runners who understand the experience

### Tone Guidelines by Context
- **Event descriptions**: Inspiring, descriptive, highlighting unique course features
- **Registration information**: Clear, practical, reassuring
- **Training content**: Supportive, expert, actionable
- **Community messages**: Warm, celebratory, inclusive
- **Safety/logistics**: Professional, thorough, confidence-building

### Writing Style Guidelines
- **Conversational and friendly**: Write as if speaking to a fellow runner
- **Customer-first language**: Focus on the runner's experience and benefits
- **Active voice preferred**: Use dynamic, energetic language
- **Clear and concise**: Respect the reader's time with scannable content
- **Professional but approachable**: Balance expertise with warmth
- **Use "you" and "we"**: Avoid third person; create connection through direct address
- **Action-oriented**: Lead with verbs and clear calls to action

## Common Event Features

### Pre-Race
- Online registration with clear pricing and event details
- Race packets with bib numbers, timing chips, and swag
- Expo areas (for larger events) with vendor booths
- Gear check and bag storage
- Clear course maps and elevation profiles

### Race Day
- Well-marked courses with mile markers
- Hydration stations at appropriate intervals
- Medical support and safety personnel
- Professional timing with chip-based results
- Spectator-friendly viewing areas
- Photographer coverage

### Post-Race
- Finisher medals for all participants
- Refreshments and recovery area
- Results available immediately via mobile app/website
- Professional race photos available for purchase
- Awards ceremonies for age group winners
- Community celebration area

## Usage Guidelines

### When generating content for Stride:
1. **Always emphasize inclusivity** - events are for all abilities
2. **Highlight the experience**, not just the race logistics
3. **Use specific, vivid descriptions** of locations and courses
4. **Balance professional information** with encouraging, warm tone
5. **Include practical details** while maintaining inspirational messaging
6. **Represent diverse runners** in descriptions and imagery
7. **Focus on the journey**, not just the finish line

### Content Structure Patterns
- **Lead with inspiration**: What makes this event special?
- **Provide practical details**: When, where, how to participate
- **Build confidence**: What to expect, how we support runners
- **Create urgency**: Registration deadlines, limited spots
- **End with clear action**: Register now, learn more, join the community
